## 🛡️ Risk Management At-A-Glance

### Stop Loss Placement
- **ATR Method:** Entry ± (2.0 × ATR)
- **Structure Method:** Recent swing high/low
- **Final Stop:** Best of both (most protection)

### Take Profit Levels (Default)
- **TP1 @ 1.5R:** Exit 30% of position
- **TP2 @ 2.5R:** Exit 30% of position  
- **TP3 @ 4.0R:** Exit final 40%

### Trailing Stop
- Activates immediately after entry
- Distance: 2.5 × ATR from current price
- Moves to breakeven at 1.0R profit
- Only moves in favorable direction

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Too Many Trades?
➜ Increase minimum confluence to 4 or 5  
➜ Enable all exit signals  
➜ Use higher timeframe charts  

### Too Few Trades?
➜ Decrease minimum confluence to 3  
➜ Add more currency pairs  
➜ Use lower timeframe charts  

### Win Rate Too Low?
➜ Increase minimum confluence  
➜ Only trade with HTF alignment  
➜ Avoid choppy/ranging markets  

### Getting Stopped Out Often?
➜ Increase ATR stop multiplier  
➜ Use structure stops  
➜ Check if market is too choppy (low ADX)  

### Not Hitting Take Profits?
➜ Reduce TP levels (1R, 2R, 3R)  
➜ Use trailing stop more aggressively  
➜ Take partial profits earlier
